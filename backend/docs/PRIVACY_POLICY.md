# Privacy Policy & Data Governance

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026  
**Platform Version:** Parallax-Eval 1.0.0 (Research & Safety Evaluation Edition)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

**Parallax-Eval** is an open-source research and engineering platform designed to empirically evaluate cross-lingual safety disparities in Large Language Models (LLMs), with an emphasis on English and Nepali (Devanagari). 

This Privacy Policy explains what data the platform processes, why it is processed, how it is stored, how third-party AI providers handle prompt data, and how users can exercise their rights to inspect, correct, or erase their data in accordance with international standards (GDPR, CCPA) and the **Nepal Privacy Act, 2075 (2018)**.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect & Process

Parallax-Eval is an AI safety evaluation tool. It is **not** designed or intended to collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII), confidential personal records, or sensitive private data.

The data processed by the platform consists of:
1. **Benchmark Prompts & Behaviors**: Text prompts in English and Nepali representing evaluated safety behaviors (e.g. cyberattacks, financial fraud, hate speech, benign queries).
2. **Target LLM Outputs**: Synthetic text completions generated by target language models in response to evaluated prompts.
3. **Automated Evaluation Annotations**: Multi-agent judge, critic, and arbiter taxonomy classifications (`SAFE_REFUSAL`, `UNSAFE_COMPLIANCE`, `BENIGN_COMPLIANCE`, `OVER_REFUSAL`), confidence scores, and reasoning text.
4. **Human Review Labels**: Optional ground-truth safety labels, comments, and reviewer identifiers entered during Rapid Human Review mode.
5. **Technical & Telemetry Metadata**: Execution latencies, token counts, estimated dollar costs, OpenTelemetry span identifiers, HTTP response codes, and guardrail intervention flags.
6. **Local Application Settings**: Target API base URLs, selected models, defense toggles, and user interface preferences stored locally on the client device.

---

## 3. Why Data is Collected (Purpose of Processing)

Data is processed strictly for the following scientific and engineering purposes:
- **RQ1 (Cross-Lingual Parity)**: Measuring whether models refuse harmful requests or fulfill benign requests with equal reliability across languages.
- **RQ2 (Multi-Agent Reliability)**: Comparing automated multi-agent safety judge verdicts against ground-truth human annotations.
- **Auditing & Reproducibility**: Providing verifiable execution traces and statistical significance evaluations (McNemar's paired chi-square test, bootstrap confidence intervals).
- **Automated Remediation**: Synthesizing identified cross-lingual vulnerabilities into Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) datasets and bilingual prompt inoculation defenses.

---

## 4. Prohibition on Submitting Sensitive Personal Data (Zero-PII Rule)

> [!WARNING]
> **Do NOT submit personal data.** Users, evaluators, and benchmark creators must **never** input real names, government identification numbers (e.g. Nepali Citizenship Numbers, National ID, Social Security Numbers), financial account details, passwords, medical records, or confidential proprietary secrets into the prompt fields.
> 
> Any synthetic names or scenarios used in benchmark prompts must be entirely fictitious. If personal data is inadvertently submitted, it will be erased immediately upon request.

## 5. Cookie Consent & Tracking Technologies Statement

### Is Cookie Consent Required?
**No.** Parallax-Eval does **NOT** use tracking cookies, targeting cookies, third-party analytics cookies, or behavioral advertising trackers.
- **Mobile Client**: The Flutter mobile application communicates directly with the backend via stateless JSON REST APIs (`http` package). It does not maintain or transmit web browser cookies.
- **Web Client (if deployed)**: If running in web mode, the application utilizes only functional local storage (`localStorage`/`sessionStorage`) strictly necessary to store user-configured API endpoints and active session state.
- **No Third-Party Ad Networks**: The platform contains no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts. Under ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC) and GDPR Article 5(3), strictly necessary functional storage does not require an intrusive cookie banner.

## 6. Third-Party AI Service Providers (BYOK Architecture)

Parallax-Eval operates on a **Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK)** architecture. When live target models are evaluated:
1. **API Keys**: User-provided API keys (OpenAI, Google AI Studio Gemini, Microsoft Azure OpenAI) are stored in local environment variables (`.env`) or securely transmitted in authorization headers (`Authorization: Bearer` or `x-goog-api-key`). They are never written to disk logs, database tables, or reverse-proxy URL query parameters.
2. **Prompt Transmission**: Prompts are transmitted directly to the respective model provider over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.
3. **Model Provider Data Retention**: 
   - **OpenAI API**: As of standard commercial API terms, data sent via the API is not used to train models and is retained for abuse monitoring for 30 days (or 0 days with an approved Zero Data Retention agreement).
   - **Google AI Studio (Gemini)**: Paid tier API calls are not used for model training. Free tier keys may be subject to Google's standard developer terms.
   - **Azure OpenAI Service**: Operates within the customer's enterprise tenant boundary with strict contractual data isolation.

## 7. Compliance with the Nepal Privacy Act, 2075 (2018)

Because Parallax-Eval specifically evaluates English-Nepali safety parity and incorporates regulatory clauses from the **Nepal Privacy Act, 2075 (वैयक्तिक गोपनीयता सम्बन्धी ऐन, २०७५)** in its Vector Policy RAG engine, the platform aligns with the statutory principles of Nepali privacy law:
- **Section 3 (Right to Privacy)**: Respect for individual dignity, personal documents, and correspondence.
- **Section 6 & 7 (Data Collection & Protection)**: Personal information cannot be collected without informed consent, and collected information cannot be processed for purposes outside the stated research scope.
- **Section 8 (Information Accuracy)**: Individuals have the statutory right to request rectification of inaccurate records.

## 8. Data Subject Rights (GDPR & CCPA Rights)

Regardless of jurisdiction, Parallax-Eval grants all contributors and evaluators full control over their data:

1. **Right to Access & Portability**: Users can export all experiments, prompt pairs, responses, and evaluation records in open formats (JSON, CSV, Markdown) via `/api/v1/experiments/{id}/export` and the developer CLI.
2. **Right to Rectification (Correct Data)**: Users can update or correct any custom prompt pair, category, or description via `PUT /api/v1/behaviors/{id}` or the mobile client interface.
3. **Right to Erasure (Delete Data)**: Users can permanently delete custom prompt pairs, behaviors, and evaluation runs via `DELETE /api/v1/behaviors/{id}` and `DELETE /api/v1/experiments/{id}`. Deletion cascades through all associated database tables, ensuring complete data expungement.
4. **Right to Object / Opt-Out**: Users may discontinue participation or delete their local database file (`parallax_eval.db`) at any time.

## 9. Age Restriction & Child Safety

The platform is strictly restricted to individuals who are **at least 18 years old** (or the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction). 
Because the platform involves red-teaming and adversarial testing of hazardous categories (cyberattacks, weapons, explicit content), minors are not permitted to use the system without accredited institutional academic supervision compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K.

---

## 10. Data Security & Storage Architecture

- **Local-First Database**: Evaluation data is stored in a local SQLite database (WAL mode) or a user-hosted PostgreSQL 16 instance.
- **Security Hardening**:
  - OWASP Secure Headers (`Content-Security-Policy`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `Strict-Transport-Security`).
  - Spreadsheet Formula Injection Neutralization (CWE-1236) on CSV exports.
  - Strict Pydantic input length validation preventing resource exhaustion attacks.
